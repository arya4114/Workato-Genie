# 🧠 PnT Genie - Intelligent Recruitment Assistant

## Overview

**PnT Genie** is an intelligent, autonomous AI assistant designed to streamline hiring workflows by automatically analyzing resumes against job descriptions (JDs), generating structured evaluations, extracting candidate information, and supporting interview scheduling.

The goal is to help recruiters and hiring managers make faster, data-driven decisions while maintaining transparency, consistency, and clear justification in every evaluation.

---

## Who This Helps

PnT Genie is built for:

* Recruiters
* Hiring Managers
* HR Teams

It reduces manual screening effort while ensuring standardized candidate evaluation.

---

## Core Capabilities

### 📄 Resume vs JD Evaluation

When a resume is uploaded:

* Automatically compares candidate qualifications with the provided JD
* Determines match quality (Good Match / Not a Good Match)
* Generates a scorecard (out of 10)
* Provides clear justification
* Lists Top 3 Pros and Top 3 Cons

This ensures every hiring decision is transparent and structured.

---

### 📊 Candidate Data Extraction

PnT Genie extracts and standardizes:

* Full name
* Professional email (.com only)
* Valid phone number formats
* Applied role
* Skills & qualifications
* Experience summary
* Education
* Past companies & tenure

If data is missing, it clearly reports **“Not Found”** instead of guessing.

---

### 📧 Automated Notifications

High-scoring candidates trigger summarized notifications for HR to accelerate shortlisting and next steps.

---

### 📅 Interview Scheduling

When requested:

* Integrates with Google Calendar
* Books interview slots
* Sends automated meeting invites with candidate details

Scheduling is only triggered intentionally to avoid unwanted automation.

---

### 🔄 Standardized Resume Conversion

PnT Genie can convert resumes into a **Jade standardized format**, automatically transforming email addresses into:

```
firstname.lastname@jadeglobal.com
```

This maintains consistent internal documentation.

---

### 💬 Intelligent User Interaction

PnT Genie can answer questions such as:

* “Does this resume match the JD?”
* “What are this candidate’s strengths?”
* “Should we shortlist?”

After evaluation, it proactively offers to generate tailored interview questions.

---

## Evaluation Framework

Every candidate assessment includes:

* Match decision
* Score out of 10
* Justification
* Top strengths
* Skill or experience gaps

No evaluation is delivered without reasoning.

---

## Behavioral Principles

PnT Genie follows strict operational rules:

* Always provide justification
* Extract only valid contact formats
* Never fabricate missing data
* Avoid triggering unrelated automation
* Clearly communicate when records are not found

Accuracy, consistency, and clarity are prioritized.
